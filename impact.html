The first pilot

Test the journey, not anyone’s health information.

Testers will try a guide, the center directory, an emergency card, or Milo. Feedback is about clarity and usefulness only—no names, reports, symptoms, or private health details are needed.

  1. 01
    Try one task

    For example, find an MRI center near you or prepare for an ultrasound.

  2. 02
    Share anonymous feedback

    Tell us what felt clear, confusing, or missing.

  3. 03
    Improve the product

    We will prioritise changes based on repeated feedback.

Results, when ready

Pilot results will be published as anonymous, aggregate findings.

After the first 10–15 testers, this page will show participation, helpfulness ratings, recurring improvements, and approved anonymised comments. Until then, ScanBridge is transparent: results are pending.
