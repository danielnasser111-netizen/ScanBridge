Emergency preparation card

When things feel urgent, a short list can help.

The emergency card helps families gather important information before heading to urgent care or the emergency department: medications, allergies, symptoms, previous reports, and questions.

If you think someone may be having a medical emergency, call the Lebanese Red Cross on 140 or go to the nearest emergency department now.

How ScanBridge began

It began with a frightening drive and no clear place to go.

ScanBridge is a youth-led health-navigation project built to make the imaging journey easier to understand, prepare for, and follow up on. It supports conversations with healthcare professionals, it never replaces them. It began after a boy nearly lost his dad while they were driving far from home. In a frightening moment, they could not quickly tell where to go or which hospital could help. The uncertainty made everything harder. ScanBridge was built so families facing an urgent or confusing medical moment can find a clearer next step, without replacing emergency services or doctors.

Our safety commitment

ScanBridge provides general educational and navigation information. It does not diagnose health conditions, interpret images or reports, recommend treatment, or replace a doctor or emergency service. For urgent symptoms or immediate concerns, seek medical care promptly.
